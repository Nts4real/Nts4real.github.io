Tool 2024
Hedonic pricing
R · Web

Car price estimator

A hedonic pricing model for the Seychelles vehicle market. The tool uses regression analysis on actual car listing data from Facebook to predict vehicle values based on characteristics including make, model, age, mileage, and condition. It allows anyone to estimate the market price of specific vehicles.
